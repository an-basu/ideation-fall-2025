# Crowdsourcing Project Idea: Collective Genius

## Author
Anika Basu (78197908)

## Problem Statement
Many smaller creators would like ideas/assets/PR to promote but can't afford larger agencies/companies. With Collective Genius, smaller creators can get more organized and hopefully credible collaboration proposals and deals. Brands also benefit with advertising through such partnerships.

## Core Concept
**One-line pitch:** Where brands and creators can crowdsources ideas/deals and collaborate.

**Target users:** Freelancers, smaller creators/marketers, brands.

**The crowd:** Same as the target users: freelancers, smaller creators/marketers, brands.

**The task:** Submitting marketing deliverables like concept sketches, promotional video clips, idea descriptions.

## Key Features
1. A collaboration/payment system
2. Templates for deliverable submission
3. A version-saving and creation system within the platform if users want to draft something there

## Feasibility Check
**Data source:** Creative portfolios, video clips, idea descriptions.

**Budget reality:** Shared folders, prize pools from sponsors, free collaboration tools.

**Crowd size needed:** Hundreds of creators and brands for many collaboration options and competition in marketing opportunities.

**Quality control approach:** Require some kind of creative portfolio/verified creator/business status for ensuring bots and scams aren't introduced.

## Technical Approach
**Human tasks:** Creative idea submission, revisions from any feedback, rating others' ideas.

**Automated tasks:** Taking in submissions, recommendation collaborations based on previous companies/creators worked with, etc.

**Aggregation method:** Open submission pools that are filtered by upvotes/downvotes for ideas, shortlisting by more experienced users, final selection by company/creator.

## Prior Work
**Similar projects:** I don't really see my idea exactly in many of the other projects but the idea of the user group and participant groups being the same is similar to StudySphere.

**Lessons from past course projects:** I learned from reviewing past projects that the crowd involved should be maximized (AKA shouldn't be too niche) but should be balanced with a specific purpose.

## Why This Could Work
I think this is like LinkedIn but more specifically for content creators and smaller brands. Thus, it builds on an idea people are familiar with but still serves a unique purpose.
